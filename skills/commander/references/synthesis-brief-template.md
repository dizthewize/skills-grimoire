**Verdict** — what that lens concluded: 🟢 supports proceeding · 🟡 proceed only with changes · 🔴 argues against.

**Evidence** — how well-supported that verdict is, carried up from the sub-brief's own Confidence line. *High* = primary sources, docs read, or probed first-hand. *Low* = thin, single-source, or inferred.

**The two columns are not the same thing, and collapsing them loses the decision.** A 🔴 at *Low* evidence is a reason to go research, not a reason to stop. A 🔴 at *High* evidence is a reason to stop. A 🟢 at *Low* evidence is the most dangerous cell on the board — it reads as permission and isn't. Where a verdict and its evidence disagree in weight, say so in §2 rather than letting the colour speak alone.
